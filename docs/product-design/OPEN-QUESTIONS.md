# OPEN QUESTIONS

## Decision labels

- VERIFIED FACT: Questions that the source cannot answer.
- PROPOSED DESIGN: Candidate framing for product decisions.
- OPEN QUESTION: Requires product-owner answer.
- ASSUMPTION: Temporary working assumption.

## 1) Core product questions

- OPEN QUESTION: Is ADRAET intended for one entrepreneur only, or for multiple users within a business?
- OPEN QUESTION: Will the product support one business per user or multiple businesses per user?
- OPEN QUESTION: Is the initial product for retail, services, manufacturing, or a broad small-business tool?
- OPEN QUESTION: Which business process is most urgent for first-time adopters: scheduling, customer tracking, stock management, or financial overview?

## 2) Functional scope questions

- OPEN QUESTION: Is the MVP primarily a dashboard and task manager, or does it need customer and inventory management from day one?
- OPEN QUESTION: Should the initial release include raw materials and cost tracking, or is this a later module?
- OPEN QUESTION: Are appointments required in the MVP, or is a task list sufficient initially?
- OPEN QUESTION: Should product pricing and inventory be tracked in the same model as a service business or as a goods business?

## 3) Security and operations questions

- OPEN QUESTION: What is the target deployment model for the product: team-managed, managed platform, or custom infrastructure?
- OPEN QUESTION: Does the product require multi-role access such as owner, manager, and employee?
- OPEN QUESTION: Are there compliance or audit requirements for business records or user activity logging?
- OPEN QUESTION: Should account recovery be email-based, manual admin reset, or both?

## 4) Data and analytics questions

- OPEN QUESTION: Which KPIs are most important for the first users: cash flow, sales, stock health, tasks completed, or customer follow-up?
- OPEN QUESTION: Should analytics be simple summaries or full reporting with date filtering and exports?
- OPEN QUESTION: Is there a requirement to generate reports for a business owner, a manager, or both?

## 5) Product assumptions to confirm

- ASSUMPTION: A single business-oriented product is more realistic than a full ERP in the first phase.
- ASSUMPTION: The current workflow and dashboard labels suggest a small-business management tool rather than a pure task app.
- ASSUMPTION: The MVP should be validated with a narrow vertical slice before expanding into multiple modules.

## 6) Immediate next decision needed

- OPEN QUESTION: Which single business workflow should define the first release: login + dashboard + tasks, or login + dashboard + customer + product operations?

This decision should be made before the architecture is locked and before the replacement system is designed.
